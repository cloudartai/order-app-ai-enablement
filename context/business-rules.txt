ORDER MANAGEMENT BUSINESS RULES

Rule 1:
An order with failed credit validation must be placed into Blocked status.

Rule 2:
Blocked orders cannot proceed to fulfillment until the issue is resolved.

Rule 3:
Orders with a value greater than 100,000 are classified as high-value orders.

Rule 4:
A blocked high-value order has a high business impact and should be reviewed by the Operations Team.

Rule 5:
Pending Validation means that one or more business validation checks are incomplete.

Rule 6:
Premium Delivery Service is considered business critical because delayed delivery may impact customer commitments.
